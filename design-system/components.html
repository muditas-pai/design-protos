Chips

Removable

The base face is white with a soft ring. Tone recolours the label and ring only; selected takes the stroke from the label colour, and neutral adds a whisper of tint to stay legible.

Tabs

One bar slides between tabs rather than each tab toggling its own border. On a tracked strip it drops 1px so the underline and the hairline read as a single line. Each label reserves its medium-weight width, so neighbours do not shift when the selection moves.

List item

With a subtitle the row top-aligns, so the leading slot sits against the title line rather than the centre of the title and subtitle together.

Brand ramp

Lightness steps evened out in OKLCH. The five marked held are exact brand anchors and never change; the rest were re-spaced around them.

Green ramp

Each step's perceived lightness is pinned to the red/blue/purple mean, so green stops reading fluorescent beside the other hues. Aliased as success-*, which is the same ramp under a second name.

Neutral ramp

The product greyscale. Note this is not Tailwind's gray: the app aliases gray to Tailwind's neutral and then overrides most steps with these values.

Hue ramps

Pinned to the exact stock ramps the app aliases. amber is Tailwind's yellow, not Tailwind's amber. Everything here is available as bg-, text- and border- utilities.
