Mudita’s protos

Creator program with HardLaunch.Every proto, and the states each one covers.

In-app users pick one of our ready-made videos, add one photo, and get a video of themselves to post. Every friend who signs up from it and makes a deck earns them 200 credits. HardLaunch does the face swap; signups are tracked on the existing Refer & earn page.

1 · The creator modal

Creator modalOpen

One modal, content on the left, the video on the right (50 : 50), one footer with the main button bottom right. Jump between steps with the review bar at the bottom.

https://muditas-pai.github.io/design-protos/explorations/mudita/creator-referral/creator-modal.html
StateWhat’s solved
How it worksThe concept in one screen: “We make the video, you post it. Earn credits for every signup.” Three steps in a card, the +200 credits card, an FAQ on likeness and photo use. The phone plays a real HardLaunch video with the face blurred and “Your photo here”.
Pick a videoRole tabs (For you, Work, Founders, Sales, Students, Teachers) with 3 or more videos each, 15 in all, as list cards. Switching tabs keeps your pick if it’s in that tab, otherwise selects the tab’s first video, so the selection is always visible. The selected video plays on the right with its script underneath; the phone scales down here to make room.
Add your faceUpload, take a selfie, or use a sample. Picking a photo is the commitment: an upload ring fills around it and the modal moves straight into making the video, no extra button.
MakingFour stages (reading your photo, matching light, syncing lips, final touches) while the phone scans; “Keep working, notify me” lets them leave.
Share (success)No footer. A four-step card: download, copy the caption (shown in full, with the referral link in it), add the link to your bio, track your signups. “Make another video” underneath.
Deep links?step=intro | pick | face | share opens on that step; &from=share adds “Back to Share” and keeps the editor behind.

Open questions

  • Yearly cap. Referral credits cap at 2,000 a year (10 friends). One good video hits that fast; do creators get a separate or higher cap?
  • Attribution. “Your video” badges and per-video signups need a link per video (e.g. ?ref=…&v=…).
  • Voice. Is it the user’s voice, the template creator’s, or synthetic? It changes the consent copy.
  • Consent and photo retention. The modal no longer has a consent checkbox. Where do likeness and 18+ consent live, and who owns the face data and the video (us or HardLaunch)?
  • Nudge copy. The engine has one title per nudge; the out-of-credits wording needs a second entry or copy keyed to the triggering event. A “first deck made” event would also help.
  • Sample video. The HardLaunch clip is linked straight from hardlaunch.com; it breaks if they move it.
