# Start from a Template: first-fold video script

Hero video for the Start from a Template feature page. Screen recording, no face, no hands.
Written for muted autoplay and loop, so the on-screen text carries it. VO is for the long cut only.

**Premise:** the structure arrives before the content does. A template is not a look, it's the set
of slides this kind of deck is supposed to have. You only supply your own context.

**Why a co-marketing partnership proposal:** it is genuinely canonical (every marketing team makes
one), it has a well-known spine, and crucially it is a deck most people make a few times a year, so
nobody has the structure memorised. That gap is the feature's whole argument. A brand guidelines
deck would not work here: everyone knows what goes in one.

## The shot that can't be cut

**0:04 to 0:10, the twelve empty titled slides.** That single frame is the entire feature. It is the
only moment in the video that shows expertise arriving before content does, and it is the thing no
competitor's "template" means. Everything after it is fill animation.

Two rules for that frame:

- **The titles must be readable at hero scale.** If the grid is too small to read `Value exchange`
  and `Audience overlap`, the shot has failed and the video is just another generation demo.
- **Do not pre-fill anything, not even the cover.** The tension only exists if the deck is visibly
  empty. Filling the cover early kills it.

## Production notes

- **Fill in place, never re-lay-out.** If slides reflow or reorder during the fill, the viewer reads
  it as "AI made a deck" instead of "the template held and my content went in". Lock the grid.
- **Stagger the fill, don't do it all at once.** Bodies landing slide by slide, roughly 150ms apart,
  reads as work happening. Simultaneous reads as a cut.
- **Show the slide count on the gallery card** (`12 slides`). It sets the expectation that a
  template is a quantity of thinking, not a visual theme.
- **Keep one slide visibly unfilled for a beat longer** than the rest, then land it. Small thing,
  makes the fill feel real rather than pre-rendered.
- **One CTA at the end**, navy-filled. No exclamation marks in any on-screen text.
- Numbers on screen should be specific and odd (`8.4K bundles`, `roughly 380K shared`). Round
  numbers read as placeholder.
- Swap **Marlow** and **Trailhead** for whatever clears legal.
