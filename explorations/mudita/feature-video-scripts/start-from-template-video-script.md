# Start from a Template: first-fold video script

40 second hero cut. Screen recording, no face, no hands. Built for muted autoplay and loop, so the
on-screen text carries it and the VO is only for the long cut.

Grounded in the JAS '26 handoff: `figma.com/design/OywpgPNGvoFAIS4haGGehy/JAS--26---Handoff?node-id=1-2798`

## The claim

A template is a position on what an excellent version of this deck answers, handed to you before
you start. The job to be done is not "give me an outline", it is *make sure this is good and I
haven't missed anything.* The fear is walking into the room having skipped something and finding
out when someone asks.

Two rules fall out of that, and they govern every line of copy:

- **Never say "we".** The template might be one the user's own team saved to govern their
  proposals. Write the authority as a property of the template, not of Presentations.ai.
- **Never show a slide count.** Counting slides drags the claim back down to mechanism. Coverage is
  the point, quantity is not.

**0:07 is the shot that cannot be cut.** It is the only moment in the product where you see what
the deck sets out to cover before any of the user's content exists. If the titles are not readable
at hero scale, the video is just another generation demo.

## What she types

The most important piece of writing in the video. Lowercase, uneven, open questions left in it. If
it reads as a well-formed prompt, the contrast dies.

```
ok trailhead partnership thoughts before i forget

their spring gear drop is apr 14, our cold brew season basically starts the
same week, feels too obvious not to do

audience, we're 1.4M monthly, they're 2.1M, overlap is maybe 380k? ask growth
for the real number

they have 40 stores and a 900k list which is the thing we actually want. we
have capacity (12k units) and the studio

idea: "the early start". 5am, first light, first mile, first cup. bundle is
their flask plus our beans

no cash either way, in kind, media value probably evens out around 180k each
side but finance needs to confirm

ridge cycles worked, 8.4k bundles in six weeks, 22% new to us, use that

legal will want 3 weeks so kickoff has to be feb

who owns this on their side? need a name before the meeting
```

## What the template makes her answer

Full content in `brand-partnership-proposal-template.md`. Bold rows are the four her braindump never
touches, which is where the template earns its place.

| Slide | The question it forces |
|---|---|
| The opportunity | Why is this worth doing, and why this quarter |
| Where our audiences overlap | Do our audiences actually meet, and where |
| What we bring | What are we putting in |
| What you bring | What are we asking of them, said plainly |
| The campaign | What is the actual creative |
| **What we would make together** | What gets made, and where does it run |
| **Timeline** | When does each side have to move |
| **How we will measure it** | How will we both know if it worked |
| The value exchange | Who puts in what, and is it even |
| We have done this before | Why should they believe we can run this |
| **Next steps** | What happens after the meeting, and who owns it |

## Production notes

- **Match the filmstrip order to the editor rail exactly.** One slide moving between those two
  shots collapses the whole argument. This is the only continuity error that matters.
- **Keep the shimmer beat.** Cutting to the editor early reads as a scene change rather than the
  same deck arriving.
- **Specific, odd numbers** (`8.4K bundles`, `roughly 380K`). Round numbers read as placeholder.
- No exclamation marks. One navy CTA at the end. Swap Marlow and Trailhead for whatever clears legal.

## Open dependencies

**The filmstrip is currently unreadable at hero scale**, small and cropped, so the shot the video
depends on does not exist yet. That panel probably wants a slide-outline list beside the thumbnails.
Worth doing regardless of the video: the outline is the reason to pick one template over another,
and right now you cannot see it before committing.

**The template has to be built.** Its copy is written: card, description and all twelve slides with
demo content are in `brand-partnership-proposal-template.md` beside this file. It does not exist in
the Marketing tab yet.

## Not in this video

The team-authored template case, where a team saves their own so every proposal they ship covers
the points they have decided matter. Stronger enterprise story, but it needs a second persona and a
governance beat that 40 seconds cannot hold. Its own video.
