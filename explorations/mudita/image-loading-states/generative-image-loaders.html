Mudita

Generative image loaders

Twelve landscape placeholders in the resolution and pixel family, the shape language generative-loaders uses for variant="resolution". Nothing loads underneath: the loader is the picture. All twelve resolve the same latent subject on the same 2.35s beat, so what you are comparing is grid, cadence, and how detail arrives. Frame and cell ratios are set separately: cells are 16:9 by default, so a pixel is a wide block rather than a square, and the column count is derived to hold that ratio.

Shortlisted so far: Resolution, Dither, Denoise and Radial. They lead the page; the other eight sit below for comparison.
