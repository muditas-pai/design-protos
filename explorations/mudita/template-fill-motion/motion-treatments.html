Mudita

A template becoming a real slide

Same slide twice: the JAS '26 partnership cover as a template (bracketed placeholders, a mosaic standing in for the picture), then the same cover with the deck's own words and its own photo. Four ways to show the model doing that work, so we can pick a house style for it. Rendered with Remotion at 1920 x 1080. The first three run seven seconds; the fourth takes ten, because it deals with one component at a time.

01

Stream

typed in, scanned down

The literal reading of generation. Placeholders evaporate upward, last character first, the block reflows as two lines collapse to one, and the real copy is typed behind a green caret. The picture resolves under a scan line travelling top to bottom. Most legible as "this text is being written for you", and the slowest of the three.

  • Character dissolve out: blur to 7px, lift 14px, reverse stagger
  • Rule and subtitle ride the reflow up 99px
  • Caret sits on whichever line is being written
  • Scan line resolves the mosaic and takes the grid with it
02

Shimmer sweep

one pass of light

One band of light crosses the slide on the background gradient's own diagonal, 140 degrees. Behind the band everything is already rewritten, ahead of it everything is still template, so text and picture change as a single gesture rather than as four separate events. Quickest to read and the easiest to reuse on any slide layout, since it knows nothing about the content underneath it.

  • Two full slide states, cross-masked on a moving gradient
  • Order falls out of the geometry: title, footer, picture
  • A bloomed core in the band, screen-blended
  • Picture border glows once as the light leaves it
03

Stagger swap

assembled piece by piece

Word by word and tile by tile. Placeholder words shrink and blur away in sequence, real words spring up from 26px below with a green flash on arrival, and the picture assembles out of its own mosaic in 368 tiles ordered from the centre outward with a little noise. The busiest of the three, and the one that most looks like a thing being built rather than a thing being crossfaded.

  • Exit 12 frames, entrance 22: leaving is always faster
  • Words arrive at 4-frame intervals, never all at once
  • Tiles are the template's own 16 x 23 grid, so nothing shifts
  • Each tile flashes brand green for three frames as it lands
04

Stream, in sequence

one component at a time

Treatment 1's mechanics with nothing running in parallel. The title finishes before the rule and subtitle are touched, those finish before the footer starts, and the picture is last. Nothing marks the current component: the caret, the rule's single pulse and the scan line do that on their own, and whatever is not its turn yet just sits at its template opacity. Clearest about what the model is doing and in what order, and three seconds slower for it, which is the trade. Best where the sequence is the point (a walkthrough, a first-run explainer) rather than a loop behind a headline.

  • Title, then rule and subtitle, then footer, then picture
  • Only one caret is ever alive, and it moves down the slide
  • Rule gets its own beat: 6% wider and a single glow
  • Each component sits at its template opacity until its turn

One file for sharing, no labels, in the order above.

Source lives beside this page in remotion/. npm run dev opens the Remotion studio to scrub a treatment frame by frame, npm run build:all re-renders all five files into remotion/out/.
