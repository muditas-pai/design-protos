Dunning & trial email copy

Email templates across two sequences. {{fields}} resolve from the tables below; text in "quotes" is literal email copy.

All of these ship on SES, the billing rail, sent from a named person rather than a marketing address.

Two acquisition variants (A/B). Group A is a free plan whose credits run out, then the user upgrades and is charged on the spot. Group B is a 7-day paid trial (card given upfront) charged at day 7. Both renew a year later, so both share the renewal sequence below.

Cadence follows the provider, not the calendar. Stripe and Paddle retry a failed charge 3 times over about a month, timed by their own algorithm for the best success rate. We send one email per failed retry (on the payment_failed webhook), so those are attempt 1 / 2 / 3, not fixed days. Access pauses at a fixed 30 days from the first failure, regardless of when the retries land.
