Overview

What is Presentations.AI?

Presentations.AI is an AI presentation platform that turns ideas, documents, and URLs into professional slide decks in minutes. It automatically applies your brand, helps create compelling content, and exports directly to PowerPoint, making it effortless to go from concept to presentation-ready deck.

Stop Rebuilding Presentations From Scratch

Use Refresh to swap in new data, prospect, or context, while your original design stays locked.

How it works

Create or import a master presentation - a high-value sales deck or a quarterly report template, for example. Set up update rules.

Connect your master to Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Sheets, Tableau, Looker, Databox, Mixpanel, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, and 50+ more. Set a schedule - daily, weekly, or monthly. When your data changes, the deck changes with it.

Take your best sales proposal. Drop in a new prospect's company, industry, pain points, and relevant context. Get a tailored version in minutes - without touching the slide structure or redesigning anything.

Define once what stays fixed - your narrative, your design, your brand - and what adapts - the data, the recipient, the numbers. Refresh handles the rest.
