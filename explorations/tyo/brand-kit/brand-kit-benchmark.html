01Pitch

Pitch

Team library

Pitch bundles brand setup under one Team library: brand tone, fonts, images, video, and templates. New templates can be auto-built from a domain — drop a URL and Pitch fetches the accent color, images, and font styling (Brandfetch-style). At creation time, the library template surfaces in the first row, in its own dedicated section.

  • Brand tone — voice guidelines that power AI actions like “Match brand tone”Free
  • FontsFree
  • ImagesFree
  • VideoPremium
  • Templates — start from an existing template/file, or create new Create new: drop the domain link → auto-fetches accent color, images & font styling. Each template gets its own dedicated file.Free
Worth stealing: domain → brand. Pasting a URL and pulling accent, imagery, and fonts in one shot is the fastest path from “nothing” to “on-brand”.
02Gamma

Gamma

Library · Themes & Custom fonts

Gamma centers everything on Themes — colors, fonts, logo, card design, and layouts, set through a multi-step builder. You can build one from scratch, import a PPT, or customize a standard theme. Uploading your own font is gated behind Pro.

  • Theme / Template — build new, or upload an existing PPT The builder steps through Colors → Fonts → Design → Logo & accents → Name.Free
  • Custom font — upload your own typefacePro
Friction noted: the theming is very complete, but it’s complex and not seamless — a 5-step wizard before you have a usable theme.
03Canva

Canva

Brand Kit Pro

Canva puts everything behind one Brand Kit surface, gated to Pro: Brand Templates, Logos, Colors, Fonts, Brand voice, Photos, Components, Graphics, and Charts — all in a single grid that applies “across designs”.

  • Brand Kit — Templates · Logos · Colors · Fonts · Brand voice · Photos · Components · Graphics · ChartsPro
04Pomelli

Pomelli

Brand DNA · Google Labs AI

Pomelli (Google Labs) builds a brand’s DNA from just a domain — research it, extract logo, fonts, colors, tagline, and values — then generates a full brand book, on-brand campaigns, photoshoots, and a website from that DNA. An agent chat walks you through or finishes it quickly.

  • Business DNA — generated from a domain (logo, fonts, color palette, tagline, brand values)
  • Brand Book — clear space, minimum size, variants, typography; download as PDF or publish
  • Campaigns — describe one or start from AI suggestions grounded in your DNA
Live example: a published Pomelli brand book — labs.google.com/pomelli/brand_book/…
05ChronicleHQ

Chronicle

Media library

Chronicle keeps it minimal — a single Media Library per workspace for shared image assets. Drop or paste JPG / PNG / GIF / SVG to upload, scoped Personal or shared.

  • Media Library — per-workspace image store; drag, drop, or paste to upload
