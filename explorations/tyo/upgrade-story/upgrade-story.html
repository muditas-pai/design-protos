04 Shared knowledge base

Selling points
  • The AI builds decks from your real company facts — revenue, overview, strategy
  • Stop re-explaining your company in every prompt
  • No more fact-checking made-up numbers
  • Your knowledge, one place the whole team draws on
Story
You upload your company's real docs once — overview, revenue, growth strategy, market segmentation. From then on every deck is grounded in your actual facts: you ask, and it pulls the right number straight from your files.
Storyboard
  1. Company files drop into "Project knowledge" — Company_Overview.pdf, Growth_Strategy_Revenue.xlsx, Market_Segmentation.ppt.
  2. They flow into a shared knowledge core the whole team can draw on.
  3. You type "Build our Q3 investor update" — the deck generates with a real figure lifted from the file, highlighted: "$4.2M ARR · from Growth_Strategy_Revenue.xlsx."
"Decks that already know your company."
Aha — the AI pulls a real number from your own file, not a guess. Grounded, not hallucinated — that's the trust moment, and what turns generic AI output into your company's deck.

05 Invite team members Refined · single frame

Selling points
  • Everyone in the same deck at the same time
  • Edit, comment, and react live
  • Decisions happen in the deck, not over email
  • A room, not an inbox of v7_final.pptx
Story
Don't cut to before/after — the magic is the live simultaneity. One slide, multiple people acting on it at once. The deck feels alive.
Storyboard — everything simultaneous
  1. Two–three named colored cursors moving at once (Sarah, Diego, You).
  2. One drags an image into place while another retypes the headline — both land live.
  3. Comment on a chart: "Can we make this bolder?" → 👍 → chart restyles in response.
  4. Presence row up top ("3 editing now") with avatars gently pulsing.
"Everyone in the same deck, at the same time."
Aha — the comment → reaction → change micro-loop. Feedback doesn't just get left, it gets acted on instantly. Give each action ~1s to breathe so the eye follows who did what.
