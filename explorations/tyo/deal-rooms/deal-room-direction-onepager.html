Our deal room should be a collaborative workspace, not a landing page

TL;DR of the full research on trumpet, Seismic, Aligned, Dock, GetAccept & Recapped · July 2026

The call: a deal room is where a seller shares everything (pitch, resources, demos, pricing) and both sides review it together, comment, and execute an agreed action plan. That's two parties doing work together — so the interface should feel like Notion/Figma (a shared workspace), not a glossy marketing microsite.

Two paradigms in the market

Showcase / landing page

trumpet, Seismic, GetAccept
  • Hero banner, brand polish, widget blocks
  • One-way: seller → buyer, buyer consumes
  • Optimized for the first 30 seconds

Collaborative workspace ← us

Aligned, Dock, Recapped
  • Sections/tabs, tasks, comments, files
  • Two-way: buyers comment, upload, own tasks
  • Optimized for the next 90 days of the deal

  1. Deals are won when the seller isn't there. 6–13 stakeholders buy asynchronously; a workspace arms the champion to run that internal process — a brochure just gets forwarded.
  2. Only work brings buyers back. Engaged rooms see ~19 visits (every 2 days); landing pages are read once. Tasks, comments, and plan updates regenerate reasons to return.
  3. The best-evidenced feature is the mutual action plan — shared tasks, owners, due dates. It's the spine of a workspace; on a landing page it's decoration.
  4. Buyers need to put things in, not just take out — security questionnaires, legal docs, procurement forms. You don't upload files to a brochure.
  5. The market agrees: workspace-first Aligned is G2's #1 DSR + #1 Client Portal (Summer 2026) and just raised a $60M Series B. Even showcase vendors are racing to add workspace features — nobody is racing to add hero banners.
  6. Workspaces outlive the signature — same room becomes onboarding & renewal. A campaign page dies at close.

The one risk to manage: don't ship something that feels like enterprise PM software. The bar is Notion-grade warmth — workspace structure + consumer-grade craft. That combo is exactly what none of the six competitors owns (Recapped is weak on design; Dock's #1 complaint is limited customization; trumpet has craft but not the workspace). That gap is our opportunity.
