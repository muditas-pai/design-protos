My Take on Deal Rooms

Why the deal room should work like a private, collaborative document workspace.

The take

A digital sales room is a persistent, private microsite shared between a seller and one specific buying committee. Instead of scattering a deal across email threads, PDF attachments, and calendar invites, everything lives behind one link: decks, demos, videos, pricing, proposals, a mutual action plan, Q&A, and often the contract itself.

I see the deal room working like a collaborative workspace — a private space between seller and buyer where the seller shares everything the deal needs: pitch deck, case studies, demo videos, even agreement docs. The buyer should be able to move through all of it effortlessly, the way you browse a well-organized Notion workspace.

That’s why I prefer this type of interface over a glossy landing-page layout. It’s simpler, it keeps the focus on the material the seller is actually sharing, and it invites real engagement — buyers leave comments, ask follow-up questions, and discuss next steps right where the content lives. The room isn’t there to impress on first open; it’s there to move the deal forward, together.

Jobs to be done

Seller / Owner

“When I’m running a deal with multiple stakeholders, I want one shared workspace holding everything — pitch, resources, demos, pricing, plan — so I can drive the whole sales cycle from a single link.”

  • Organize materials so buyers actually review them, not lose them in an inbox
  • Equip the champion to sell internally when I’m not in the room
  • See who engaged with what — read deal health, follow up on signal
  • Agree on a shared action plan with owners and dates to keep momentum
  • Carry the same workspace into onboarding after close

Buyer / Client

“When I’m evaluating a solution with my team, I want everything the seller shared in one easy-to-navigate place, so I can review it on my own time and share it internally.”

  • Comment or ask questions right on the material — answers in context, not more email
  • Pull colleagues into the same space so everyone aligns on the same information
  • Know the next steps and who owns them via a clear shared plan
  • Hand over docs (security questionnaires, procurement) in the same workspace
  • A simple, focused document space — no marketing theater — straight to substance
