Key rules

🎁 Rewards are credits onlyNo cash, no payout, no points, no discount. Friend or visitor makes a first deck: both get 200 credits. Up to 2,000 a year per person, shared by referral links and templates. Paid plan: +1,000 credits when they move to a monthly or yearly plan within 7 days and stay paid 30 days, up to 10,000 a year.

🌍 Publishing

  1. Share > Publish to Community > Publish now > Details > Live. No mask step
  2. No page yet: Create page first (Step 1 of 2), then Details (Step 2 of 2)
  3. Page cover: not asked when creating, a default abstract cover is used. Change it later in Edit page (upload or pick a colour)
  4. Unpublish never removes copies people already made

🏢 Company community

  1. Every company page exists, unclaimed, built from what teammates published
  2. Claim with a work email and a 6 digit code. Personal emails can't claim
  3. Claimer becomes admin. Verified domain badge
  4. Admin decides: members can publish, and review before live
