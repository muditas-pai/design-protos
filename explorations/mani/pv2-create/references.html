Tier and effort pickers

For the Budget tier menu (Automatic, Fast, Efficient, Standard, Premium). Two icon families below, drawn in our 16px 1.5 stroke style, on the real menu layout. Then the screens they come from.

A · Level bars (recommended)

One family shows the level: 1, 2, 3 bars for Efficient, Standard, Premium. Automatic gets the sparkle, Fast gets the bolt. Same idea as Replit's Lite / Economy / Power icons getting denser and Gamma's speed meter.

B · Semantic

A picture per tier: leaf for Efficient, scales for Standard, diamond for Premium. Reads well alone, but the five icons do not line up as a scale, so the order is harder to feel. Twenty and Relevance AI use this style.

Deep search toggles

How chat tools surface their research mode in the prompt box. Four patterns in the wild, none of them a bare switch.

1. Toggle pill in the toolbar (recommended)

A chip with an icon and a one word label. Tap turns it on: tinted fill, brand text. Headline and placeholder change to match ("What are you researching?", "Get a detailed report"). This is what v2 A and B do.

ChatGPT
2. Removable mode chip

Picked from the + menu, then sits in the box as a tinted chip with an x. The box prefills "Create a research report on". Clear with the x.

Copilot
3. Mode dropdown

One "Search" chip with a caret. Menu swaps the mode: Search, Research, Labs. Telescope icon for Deep research. Good when modes are exclusive, worse for a yes or no extra.

Perplexity

Claude hides Research inside the + menu as a checked row, next to a Web search toggle. Fine for power users, invisible for first timers. Telescope is the common icon for research (ChatGPT, Perplexity). Copilot shows the remaining quota on the menu row ("5 remaining"), which maps to our credits per slide line.
