Options popover, three layouts

Every setting from the live PV2 dropdown, built on the previous layout you liked. Budget tier and Deep search stay in the toolbar. Click around: the drill-in, tabs and toggles work.

What went wrong in the last version
  • Slide count looked different from the fields around it: an icon chip and a side caret next to plain dropdowns.
  • "Advanced options" hid five of the nine settings behind a collapse with no hint of what is inside.
  • Level of detail lost its one-tap segmented control and became a dropdown.
  • Opened, the menu became a tall list of identical dropdowns. Nothing stood out.
A · Previous layout + Story drill-in (recommended)

The menu you liked, with one extra row. Slide count, language, role, detail and speaker notes stay exactly as before. A "Story and audience" row opens the four new settings, the same drill-in pattern Slide count already uses. The row shows what is set, so nothing is hidden by surprise.

B · Two tabs

Basics and Story side by side. Same short height as before. The Story tab shows a count when something is set. Tabs make the extra settings easy to find, but most people never need the second tab.

C · All visible, grouped

Nothing hidden. Three small groups: Deck, Content, Story. Short choices become segmented controls (Missing data, Detail) so the menu is not a wall of dropdowns. It still scrolls, so it is the heaviest option.
