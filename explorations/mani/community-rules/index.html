Publish to Community v3

Example: Joe works at Foursquare. He publishes his deck KPI Report as a template. Priya finds it and signs up. Hand-off for engineering: every rule and scenario in the proto.

✂️ What changed: no mask stepShare > Publish to Community now goes straight to Details. There is no "mask sensitive data" screen. Creators check their own deck (the Community tab tells them to), every publish is checked automatically for unsafe content, and anyone can report a template that shows private data.
🎁 Rewards are credits onlySame rules as Refer and earn. Every reward is AI credits. No cash, no payout, no points.

The rules

👤 Community page

  • One page per person: presentations.ai/@handle
  • Handle: at least 3 characters, up to 24, must be free ("That address is taken")
  • Display name (40), headline (60), bio (160), photo
  • Cover: not asked when creating. Every new page gets an abstract colour cover. Later, Edit page lets people upload a wide image (cropped to 3:1) or pick one of 5 colours
  • What do you make: pick up to 3 topics
  • Links are optional: LinkedIn, X, website, more
  • Made from the dashboard (Community tab) or on the way in Share

📝 Details step

  • Title, required, up to 60. Publish stays off while empty
  • Description up to 600
  • Category: Reports, Marketing, Sales, Startups, Product, Finance, Design, Education
  • Tags: up to 5, 20 characters each
  • Shows the Deck Score. 75 or more is featured in the gallery
  • Publishing as: Joe's page or Foursquare's team page

🔁 After it's live

  • Joe edits the deck: Share shows "You edited this deck after publishing" and Review and update
  • Update goes to Details, button reads Publish update
  • Edit details or Unpublish from Share or Manage template
  • Unpublish: it leaves Community and his page. People who used it keep their copy
  • Joe can publish it again any time

🎁 Rewards

  • Sign up from a template, then first deck: both get 200 credits
  • Creator limit: 2,000 a year, shared with his referral link. Resets 1 Jan
  • Paid creator: +1,000 credits when a visitor moves to a monthly or yearly plan within 7 days and stays paid 30 days. Up to 10,000 a year
  • Weekly plans, refunds and later upgrades don't count

🏢 Team page

  • Every company already has a page, unclaimed, built from what teammates published
  • Claim with a work email (@foursquare.com) and a 6 digit code. Personal emails can't claim
  • The claimer becomes admin. Page gets a Verified domain badge
  • Admin settings: logo, name, address, about, links, banner
  • Toggles: Members can publish here, Review templates before they go live
  • Admin can unclaim. Wrong claim: Contact support

🛡 Trust and safety

  • Every publish is checked automatically for unsafe content
  • Licence on every template: free to use and adapt, with credit to the creator
  • Report this template: private data, copies someone's work, pretends to be someone, spam, unsafe
  • The creator never sees who reported
  • Badges come from the Presentations.AI team, never automatic

User stories

1. ✍️ Joe has a page, publishes a deck

  1. Joe opens KPI Report, clicks Share, then Publish to Community
  2. He sees 200 credits per sign up, +1,000 per upgrade on paid plans, and clicks Publish now
  3. Details opens. He checks the title, picks Reports, adds tags, clicks Publish
  4. "KPI Report is live." A ready post with his link, for LinkedIn, X and Facebook

Result: live on Community and on @joe. One step only.

2. 🆕 Joe has no page, publishes from Share

  1. Share, Publish to Community, Publish now
  2. Create your community page (Step 1 of 2). He adds a name, handle and photo
  3. Toast: "Your page is live at presentations.ai/@joe". Details opens (Step 2 of 2)
  4. Publish

Result: page and template go live together.

3. 🏠 Joe makes his page first, from the dashboard

  1. Dashboard, Community tab: "Your community page." with how it works
  2. Create your page. Page is live but empty: "Publish your first template"
  3. Later he opens a deck and publishes like story 1

Result: same publish flow, page already there.

4. 🔁 Joe edits a live template

  1. He changes slides after publishing
  2. Share, Community tab: "You edited this deck after publishing"
  3. Review and update opens "Update template details". Publish update

Result: new version live. Older copies people made stay as they were.

5. 🙋 Priya signs up from the template

  1. Opens presentations.ai link, sees the template page, Deck Score and licence
  2. Use this template. Sign up: "New here? 200 bonus credits after your first deck. You're starting from KPI Report by Joe"
  3. Picks her role (Step 1 of 2), adds her own notes, file or link, or skips (Step 2 of 2). 10 credits a slide
  4. Deck is made, she presents, opens the editor: "200 credits added!"

Result: Priya has her own copy and her own referral link. Joe gets +200.

6. 🏢 Joe claims Foursquare's page from settings

  1. Workspace settings, Public gallery: Unclaimed. Claim this page
  2. Work email joe@foursquare.com, Send code, enter the 6 digits
  3. "You're the admin of Foursquare's page. Domain verified"

Result: team page live with teammates' templates, Verified domain badge.

7. 🏢 Joe publishes as the team, page not claimed

  1. Details, Publishing as: Foursquare, "Claim it to post here"
  2. Button reads Claim page and publish. Claim opens (Step 2 of 2)
  3. Verify, claim and publish

Result: page claimed and template live on it.

8. ⏳ Member posts, review is on

  1. Admin turned on Members can publish and Review before live
  2. A member publishes to their own page with "Also show on Foursquare's team gallery" on
  3. Bell: "waiting for approval". Admin sees "1 template waiting for review", Approve

Result: "Approved. It's live on the team gallery". Live on their own page right away.

Quick answers

Do we hide private data for the creator?No. The mask step is gone. The creator checks the deck, we check for unsafe content, people can report.
Does unpublishing delete copies?No. People who used it keep their copy.
Does the creator need a paid plan?No. Anyone can publish and earn 200 per sign up. Upgrade credits need a paid plan.
Is the template limit separate from referrals?No. One 2,000 credit limit a year for both.
Can anyone post to a team page?Only if the admin lets members publish. Review is optional.
What is proto only?The flow bar, toasts for social posts and the 6 digit code (any 6 digits work).
