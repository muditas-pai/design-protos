Flow (the current live flow, now with monthly)

  1. Plans with a Yearly / Monthly switch at the top.
  2. Close the plans: the instant offer pops up straight away (10% off the first year, one-hour timer).
  3. Apply it: the yearly checkout with the offer pill, struck price and Save line.
  4. Buy a yearly plan without an offer and close the checkout: "Before you go, 10% off for the next hour." applies in place.
  5. Monthly plans check out at full price; the instant offer is yearly only.

Checkout rules (same as the Question discount page)

  • Only the plan being bought, in a plain white card: no radio, no selection styling, no other plans (plan changes live in Manage your plan).
  • No order summary. Right side is the live card form.

How it works

1. Plans

  • Free account opening the plans: Yearly / Monthly switch at the top, Yearly selected ("Save up to 20%"). US: Pro and Gold; no Basic. No Team plan (3 Nov update): Gold is the team plan, same price and credits, plus Unlimited collaboration. Gold shows an "Unlimited collaboration" tag next to its title (pricing cards, Manage your plan, checkout) and lists it first under "Everything in Pro".
  • Yearly cards show "Save US$X a year vs monthly". Monthly cards show "Billed monthly. Unused credits carry over."

2. Instant offer on close

  • First close of the plans, free accounts only: the instant offer pops up straight away, "Save 10% on your first year" (PRESENT10, CLOSE_COUPONS.pricing) with a one-hour timer (CloseNudgeV2).
  • Apply 10% discount opens the yearly checkout for Pro (or the chosen plan) with the offer pill, the struck price and "Save". Close on the offer closes the plans.
  • While the offer runs, the yearly plan cards show it (struck monthly price, "US$216 first year, then US$240", "Save US$24 on your first year (10% off)").

3. Checkout

  • Buy on a yearly card opens the yearly checkout for that plan. If no offer is running, the first close applies the same 10% in place: "Before you go, 10% off for the next hour." (CheckoutScreenV2 handleClose). Closing again returns to the plans, now showing the offer.
  • The offer is yearly only: monthly cards never show it and Buy on Monthly opens the monthly checkout at full price.
  • Offers never stack; the in-place offer is the same 10% tier as the instant offer.
  • Checkout rules: only the plan being bought, plain white card, no radio, no other plans, no order summary; right side is the live card form.

4. After the hour

  • The timer ends the offer and the cards go back to full price. The live bottom-right "last call" reminder (useOfferReminderV2) is not part of this handoff.

5. Paid accounts

  • Same Manage your plan as the Question discount handoff: up only, no yearly to monthly, Gold is the top plan, change starts today, unused credits carry over, one-click payment.

6. Backend

  • Monthly Pro / Gold SKUs (prices and credits). The offer resolves and claims on the server as live (resolveCloseOffer / applyCloseBump); the local build runs it client-side on the US preview catalog.
