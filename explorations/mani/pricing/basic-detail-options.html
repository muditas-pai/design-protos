Basic is priced to convert, not to maximise revenue. Its job is to turn price-sensitive free users into paying customers. Success is measured on 90-day revenue per eligible signup and 12-month cohort revenue, not on Basic ARPU.

The one deliberate limitation

Basic cannot buy additional credits. When the 1,000 run out, the only route forward is Pro. That is intentional: it makes Basic an upgrade engine rather than a destination.
