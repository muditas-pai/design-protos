Still to decide To decide

Product decisions. The protos use the default noted in each.

Monthly gives more credits than yearlyPro monthly is 1,000 a month (12,000 a year) against 5,000 on Pro yearly; Gold monthly is 60,000 a year against 50,000. So "save $60 a year" also means 7,000 fewer credits. The monthly credit numbers (from the app fallbacks) need fixing, or yearly needs more credits.
Weekly is cheaper per credit than yearlyWeekly is about $0.030 a credit, yearly $0.048. The mobile paywall no longer claims a saving on yearly. Raise the weekly price, or keep it as is on purpose.
No proration on monthlyFull price for the new plan, unused credits carry over, unused days are lost. The app (Chargebee) shows a "Pro rate" credit today. Pick one.
Weekly credits after the week endsKeep them or drop them when the weekly plan runs out? The protos keep them during the week and show Free credits after.
Credit packs for weekly usersZero credits offers a pack to weekly users. Sell packs on weekly, or only point to monthly?
Pack checkout for paid usersThe pack checkout keeps the card form from zero-credits.html. Paid users have a card on file, so it could be one-click like Manage your plan.
DowngradesGold to Pro, yearly to monthly, team to individual are not offered. Today: cancel renewal, then buy the smaller plan after the end date.
Seats on monthly and weeklyIndividual plans are one seat. Inviting a teammate should point to Pro Team or Gold Team. Confirm.
Renew earlymanage-plan lets yearly users renew early by picking the current plan. v2 shows the current plan without a radio. Keep renew early for yearly?
20% renewal discount plus an upgradeDoes the discount carry to the new plan?
GETGOLD couponThe app applies it when Pro moves to Gold. Does Pro monthly to Gold get it?
Team: buy more credits, Pro Team to Gold Teampricing-rules.md allows both; the Team billing page has no button.
Expired monthly or weeklyRenew now gives returning subscribers 20% off. Same for monthly and weekly?
RefundsWindow and rule, for example a weekly user who buys yearly by mistake.
Seven Day Pass or weekly in the close flowThe pass step stays for now. Should it sell the weekly plan instead?
Public pricing pageDoes it show monthly and weekly, and mark the current plan for a logged-in user? No v2 built yet.
PDF export and team plans on mobileThe export modal has no PDF entry, and there is no mobile team screen. Both need a base screen first.
