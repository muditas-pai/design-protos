The rules

What Change plan offers from each plan. Plans only move up.

Current planCan move toHow billing works
Pro monthly newPro yearly (save $60 a year), Gold monthly, Gold yearly, TeamChanges in place. Starts today, credit for the unused days of the month, unused credits carry over.
Gold monthly newGold yearly (save $120 a year)Same as Pro monthly. No Pro: that would be a step down.
Pro weekly newBuys a new plan: Pro or Gold monthly, Pro or Gold yearly, TeamSeparate plan, so nothing converts. Full price, no credit for the week. The weekly renewal stops so they are never charged for both. Unused credits stay.

Pro yearlyGold yearly, TeamIn place. No yearly to monthly.
Gold yearly, TeamNo Change plan button (as in v1)Cancel renewal only. Team upgrades go to sales.
  • The new plan starts today and its year or month restarts from today, so the renewal date moves.
  • Cancelling a monthly or weekly plan has no 20% offer step. That offer is worked out on a yearly price.
  • Prices for monthly ($25 Pro, $60 Gold) and weekly ($2.99, 100 credits) are placeholders. The app reads real prices from getAllPlans.

To decide open

  1. Real monthly and weekly prices. $25, $60 and $2.99 for 100 credits are placeholders.
  2. Weekly credits on a new plan. The proto keeps them. If weekly truly connects to nothing, they could be dropped instead.
  3. Gold to Team. pricing-rules.md allows Gold to Gold Team, but Gold has no Change plan button. Gold monthly only offers Gold yearly to match.
  4. A save offer for monthly cancels. Today none. "Switch to yearly and save" could fill that step.
  5. Close flow wording. The close flow sells "one month, then yearly" and a Seven Day Pass that "counts toward a yearly plan". Both need to match a standalone monthly plan and a weekly plan that connects to nothing.
  6. Engineering gap. In the app today, a weekly user on desktop still sees Upgrade and goes to Pro to Gold. It has to switch to the new-plan flow.
