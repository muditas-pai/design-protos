Scenarios we had not covered To decide

Not built yet. Each needs a decision before engineering.

Weekly bought in the mobile appIn the app code, weekly is sold on mobile only. If it was paid through the App Store or Google Play, the web cannot stop that renewal. The user has to cancel in the store, or they pay for both.
One-click payment failsCard declined on Confirm Payment. manage-plan has no error state. Options: inline error under the card row, then Change card.
No card on fileOne-click needs a saved card. A weekly user from mobile may not have one on the web, so the right side needs the full card form (the Stripe panel in manage-plan).
No proration on monthlyA Pro monthly user 12 days into the month pays the full $240 for yearly. Unused credits carry over, but the unused days are lost. The app (Chargebee) shows a "Pro rate" credit today. Pick one.
Weekly credits when the week endsThe weekly plan runs to its end date. Do its unused credits stay after that, or end with it?
DowngradesGold to Pro, yearly to monthly, team to individual are not offered. What should a user who asks for one see? Today: cancel renewal, then buy the smaller plan after the end date.
Renew early from Manage your planIn manage-plan the current plan card is selectable (Renew Silver). For monthly and weekly that makes no sense, so v2 shows the current plan without a radio. Keep it for yearly?
Upgrading while the 20% renewal discount is appliedDoes the discount carry to the new plan or drop?
Team: buy more credits, Pro Team to Gold Teampricing-rules.md allows both, but the Team billing page has no button.
Expired monthly or weeklyRenew now opens the plans with 20% off for returning subscribers. Same for monthly and weekly?
Prices to confirmPro monthly $25, Gold monthly $120 (5,000 credits), weekly $2.99 for 100 credits are placeholders. Gold uses pricing-rules.md ($1,200, 50,000 credits); the app config says 25,000.
Close flow wordingIt sells "one month, then yearly" and a Seven Day Pass that "counts toward a yearly plan". Both conflict with a standalone monthly plan and a weekly plan that connects to nothing.
