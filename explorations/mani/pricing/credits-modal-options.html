Metaphor

Receipt, wallets, coupons

Leans fully into money language. The 170 is a till receipt with perforated edges, plans are wallets with a balance, earning is a stack of coupon stubs with the amount on the tear-off.

  • Most memorable, very shareable in a deck
  • Monospace numbers feel "counted"
  • Wallet colours reuse the plan card palette

Scannable

Bento grid

Everything on one screen, no scrolling, no tables. A dark hero tile owns the 170, three small tiles carry the unit costs, a blue tile places the plan on the ladder, the earn list and the free ceiling get their own cards, and "when it runs out" is three short tiles.

  • Fits in the modal without scroll
  • Tiles can be reordered per plan (Gold sees the gold tile first)
  • Easiest to lift into marketing later
