How credits work

Credits are one currency for every AI action. What you spend:

ActionCreditsNotes
Chat message1Each message while refining a deck
Standard slide10A topic, document or URL turned into slides
Planning or research slide20Web research costs roughly 2x
Finished presentation100About 10 slides. Longer decks cost proportionally more
Credits last your whole plan year. There is no monthly reset. A quiet month is never wasted, and a busy month is never blocked.

How to upgrade

Open Plans and billing

From your dashboard, open Settings, then Plans and billing. Or click Upgrade anywhere you see it in the editor.

Pick Individual or Teams

Individual shows Basic, Pro and Gold. Teams shows Team and Enterprise. Not ready for a plan? Choose the Presentation Pass for one week of Pro.

Choose your plan and pay

Click Buy on the plan you want, enter payment details and confirm. Plans are billed annually. Your plan and credits activate immediately.

Invite your team

On Team, go to Settings, then Members, and invite by email or share a link. Everyone joins at no extra cost and draws from the shared pool.

Upgrading from Basic to Pro? Your remaining Basic credits carry over. Upgrades apply immediately and you pay only the difference for the rest of your year.

FAQs and common issues

Can I switch between plans?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and you pay the prorated difference for the rest of your billing year. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep full access until then.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings, then Plans and billing, and click Manage subscription. You keep your plan, features and credits until the end of the period you have paid for. After that your workspace moves to Free. Your decks stay yours.

Is there monthly billing?

Plans are billed annually. The monthly figure on the pricing page ($4, $20, $50, $60) is the effective monthly rate. If you only need a short burst, the Presentation Pass gives you 7 days of Pro for $19.99 with no subscription.

What is the Presentation Pass?

A one-time purchase: 700 credits and every Pro feature for 7 days, $19.99, never renews. Enough for about 7 finished decks. Unused credits expire when the pass ends.

What if several people share a workspace?

Basic, Pro and Gold are for one person. If several people need to make decks, the Team plan covers everyone at one price, $720 a year, with unlimited seats. Adding a colleague costs $0.

Do credits roll over?

Credits on Basic, Pro, Gold and Team are valid for your whole plan year, so there is nothing to roll over month to month. At renewal you receive a fresh year of credits. Free and earned credits have their own expiry, shown in the earning table above.

How do I get more credits?

On Pro and Gold, buy a credit pack from Plans and billing. On Team, expand the shared pool from $200 per 10,000 credits. On Basic, upgrade to Pro. On Free, earn credits or take a Presentation Pass.

Why is a finished deck 100 credits?

A typical deck is about 10 slides at 10 credits a slide. Research slides cost 20. Chat while refining costs 1 credit a message. Longer decks cost proportionally more. This is a working average, not a metered fee.

What is the difference between Pro and Gold?

Gold adds frontier AI models (the best available), advanced brand customization, Projects with a shared knowledge base, and 3x the credits (15,000 vs 5,000) for 2.5x the price. Choose Gold when the quality of the model matters more than the price of the seat.

Which plans include PowerPoint export?

Every paid plan and the Presentation Pass export editable .pptx files with real, editable text, shapes and charts. Free can present and share online only.

What happens to my credits after I cancel?

Your paid credits stay usable until the end of the period you paid for. After that the workspace moves to Free. Any earned free credits keep their own expiry.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Enterprise contracts include a written guarantee. We are SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant on all plans.
