Pricing modal · reference board

For the post-export "Where does this deck go next?" modal: testimonial rail on the left, Single PPT vs Pro cards on the right, customer logos in the footer. 32 screens from Mobbin grouped by what each one teaches, with the exact element worth borrowing. Every card links to the Mobbin page.

What our modal is made of

  • ATestimonial rail: big serif quote, avatar, name, role, stars.
  • BTwo plan cards: one-time Single PPT vs Pro subscription, "Most popular" ribbon, feature checklist with icons.
  • CLogo footer: EY, BCG, Amazon, Meta, Google, McKinsey, Microsoft, Notion, Adobe.
  • DContext: it opens after an export, so the deck is the hero of the moment.

Takeaways from the set

  • Heidi and Square are the closest matches: quote + Trustpilot / metrics beside the offer. Put a rating or a number under the quote, not just stars.
  • Emergent and Salesforce label the cards by state ("Recommended", "You're currently using"). Label Single PPT as "This deck only" and Pro as "Every deck, all year".
  • Juicebox and Uxcel put the logo strip inside the modal with a line of copy ("Trusted by 25,000+ recruiters"). Add "Loved by 12M+ presenters at" above our logos.
  • Contra and Vercel show a "thanks / welcome" state in the same modal. Design the post-purchase state now, not later.
  • Docusign and Gamma keep a billing toggle in the header. If Pro gets a monthly price, that is where it goes.
