# Pricing v7.2: equal-height cards, credit emphasis, upgraded Teams row

Developer handoff for `explorations/mani/pricing/v7.2.html`. v7.2 builds on v7 (plan-count tabs) and v7.1 (credit badges + divider). Everything not listed here behaves as in the v7 handoff (`V7_HANDOFF.md`): plan-count tab, card positions and seam overlap, pricing, tooltips, compare table, 25%-off hour bump, close-intent popup.

## What's new vs v7

### Credit counts (all three Individual cards)
- The number is 26px extra-bold (`.cred-num`), solid navy `#0A1925` on Basic and Pro.
- Gold's number (`.cred-num--gold`) has a slow-shimmer gold gradient; Gold's sparkle icon is gold `#c9932a`.
- Sparkle icon next to a credit count renders at 22px.
- Multiplier badge (`.xbadge`) after the count: Pro shows "5x Basic" (Brand Blue tint, `id="pro-x-badge"`, visible only on the 3-card tab), Gold shows "10x Pro" (gold tint, both tabs).
- The credits row (`li.cred-row`) is baseline-aligned flex with 14px bottom padding and a 1px `#e5e5e5` divider below it.

### Equal-height cards
- All visible cards in a row share the height of the tallest (script sets `min-height`).
- Two groups equalize independently: Individual (`#basic-card`, `#pro-card`, `#gold-card`) and Teams (`#team-pro-card`, `#team-gold-card`, `#team-ent-card`).
- Recalculates on tab clicks, billing-period toggles, and window resize. Mobile (≤768px) keeps natural heights.

### Auto-sized card spacer
- The in-flow spacer (`#card-spacer`) below the card area sizes itself to the lowest visible card bottom + 24px (ResizeObserver + resize listener). Never hand-tune its height. Hidden on mobile.

### Feature list changes (Individual)
- Pro: new last point "Turn any deck into a native LinkedIn carousel" (ph-linkedin-logo).
- Gold: same LinkedIn point inserted below "Audience report on how each deck performs" (before "Early access to beta features").
- Basic card illustration is a plane image (embedded base64, `.illus-basic`).

### Teams row (mirrors the Individual highlight logic)
- **Gold Team is the emphasized card**: 2px border, rounded 4px, shadow, `z-index:3`, at `left:382px` so its border covers Pro Team's right edge (2px seam overlap).
- **Pro Team de-emphasized**: 1px `border-brand-secondary`, rounded, shadow, `top:0` (no more floating up 21px), `z-index:1`, content-hugging with 60px bottom padding.
- **Enterprise** sits at `left:742px` with `border-l-0`, tucked under Gold's border; `min-height:680px` as a floor, then equalized.
- **Pro Team points** (label "All Free features, plus:" removed): Unlimited seats first, then the full Individual Pro list: Advanced AI models and agents · Export to PowerPoint and Google Slides · Create slides from PPT import, up to 60 slides · Brand kit: Use your brand's font, colors and logo · Full premium template library · Organize decks into projects · View tracking & analytics · Turn any deck into a native LinkedIn carousel.
- **Gold Team points** (label "All Pro features, plus:" removed): Unlimited seats first, then the full Individual Gold list: Ultra AI model, our most powerful · Create slides from PPT import, up to 99 slides · Export to PowerPoint and Google Slides · Projects with a shared knowledge base (tooltip) · Refresh data across decks in one click (tooltip) · Custom brand kit and moods, built for you (tooltip) · Full premium template library · Advanced tracking and analytics · Audience report on how each deck performs · Turn any deck into a native LinkedIn carousel · Early access to beta features · Priority support, with direct access to our Head of Product.
