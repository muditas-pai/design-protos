Pass plan · Mobbin references

Patterns for a fixed-duration pass: full plan access for X days, one payment, no auto-renewal. Three families below: true duration passes, time-boxed capacity that expires, and "pay once, no subscription" framing.

What to steal for our 7-day pass

  • Name the duration in the product (Careem "One day pass", Saily day picker): "7-day pass" beats "trial" or "short plan".
  • Total price, not per-day math (Blackbird): one number for the whole window.
  • "No auto-renewal" as the trust line (Peanut "One time payment. No subscription"): put it directly under the CTA, not in fine print.
  • State the window plainly (Lime "Use within 7 days", Midjourney expiry note): say when it ends and what happens after (nothing is charged).
  • Sell it beside the plans, not instead of them (Open, Liven): the pass is a row or card next to Pro, with an upgrade path when it ends.
