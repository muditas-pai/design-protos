Pass plan · Mobbin references (web)

Web-only patterns for a fixed-duration pass: full plan access for X days, one payment, no auto-renewal. Two families: plans sold by fixed length, and one-time purchases that expire instead of renewing.

A · Plans sold by fixed duration

Hims Web

Fixed-length plans

8-week / 16-week / 6-month plans as the tier choice, each with a plan breakdown and total charged once. Closest web pattern to "full plan for X weeks", with the window stated inside the card.

Wix Web

Duration picker

"Select a Billing Cycle" radio list: Monthly / Yearly / 2 Years / 3 Years, each a fixed total paid up front with a save chip. Clean radio-row model for choosing how long the access window runs.

NordVPN Web

Length first

"Choose length for your new plan": the duration (2-year / 1-year / monthly) is picked before the tier, with the total for the whole period under the per-month price. Duration as the primary decision.

B · One-time purchases that expire (no renewal)

Midjourney Web

Expiring capacity

Buy Fast Hours: one-time confirm modal with price breakdown and an explicit expiry note ("expire after 60 days"). Model for the confirm step of a pass purchase, no renewal language anywhere.

What to steal for our 7-day pass

  • Name the duration in the product (Hims 8-week plan, NordVPN length-first): "7-day pass" as the tier name, not "trial" or "short plan".
  • Total price for the window, charged once (Wix, Hims): one number, no per-day math, no "/mo".
  • Cadence label right next to the price (GitHub "one time"): the non-renewing nature is part of the price line itself.
  • Expiry stated plainly near the CTA (Midjourney, Upwork): say when it ends and that nothing is charged after.
  • Sell it beside the plans (Open, GitHub): the pass is a card or row next to Pro, with an upgrade path when it ends.
