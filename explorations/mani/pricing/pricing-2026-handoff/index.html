How it works

1. Who sees what

  • Free account opening the plans (Upgrade, See plans, out of credits, Gold feature): the Question discount pricing page, yearly plans only (US: Pro and Gold; no Basic).
  • Paid account: the plans are plan-aware (Current plan / Change plan / Not available on your plan) and every Change plan opens Manage your plan. Paid accounts never see the close questions.

2. Closing the plans: "What stopped you?"

  • Shown on the first close of the plans in each opening, free accounts only. Closing again just closes. X on any step ends the flow.
  • Each answer has its own steps; "No thanks" moves to the next one:
AnswerSteps
The price is too high10% off the first year → Pro monthly → closed
I don't want to pay for a whole yearPro monthly → closed
I'm not sure the credits are enoughWhat the credits cover (Back to plans returns to the plans, question not asked again) → Pro monthly → closed
The deck isn't good enough yetFix the deck first: Deck Score, Edit the outline, Hire an expert. No discount.
Just looking for now"No problem", closed. No offer.

3. Offers and checkout

  • 10% off is the only discount: coupon PRESENT10 (CLOSE_COUPONS.pricing), first year only, yearly plans only, one hour. Apply opens the yearly checkout for the chosen plan with the "10% off · Ends in MM:SS" pill, the struck price and "Save".
  • Start Pro monthly opens the monthly checkout at full price. Its No thanks closes the flow (no Weekly Pass step).
  • On this page, closing a checkout just closes: there is no in-place "Before you go" offer (that belongs to the Instant discount page). Offers never stack.
  • Checkout rules: only the plan being bought, plain white card, no radio, no other plans, no order summary; the right side is the live card form (Chargebee fields, Reach). Plan changes live in Manage your plan.

4. Monthly plans

  • Pro monthly and Gold monthly renew every month; unused credits carry over. Cancel any time; access runs to the end of the month paid for.
  • Cancel renewal on monthly says "next month" and has no 20% offer step (the 20% stay offer is yearly only).
  • Dashboard nudge "Switch to yearly and save X" (US$60 Pro, US$240 Gold); dismiss hides it for a week; it opens Manage your plan with the yearly plan selected.
  • Payment failed: 7-day grace with a red banner and Update card; after the grace period the workspace is on Free and decks stay saved.

5. Weekly Pass

  • Its own plan, not tied to Pro or Gold: 7 days, every feature except collaboration, one payment, nothing renews. The user can move to Pro monthly or yearly any time.
  • Settings: "US$2.99 for 7 days · Ends on DATE. Nothing renews." No Cancel renewal. "Get a monthly or yearly plan" opens Choose your new plan; the pass is not upgraded, it runs to its end date.
  • Dashboard: "Keep going after your Weekly Pass" while active, "ends on DATE" in its last day, "has ended" after (back on Free, decks saved).

6. Manage your plan (paid accounts)

  • Current plan card on top (selectable only to renew a yearly plan early), Monthly / Yearly switch, the allowed moves as cards with the selected plan's points inside, one-click payment on the saved card.
  • Rules: up only; no yearly to monthly; weekly and expired plans pick a fresh plan; Gold is the top plan, so Gold yearly has no move (renew early only). Invite on a one-seat plan (Pro, Weekly Pass) opens Gold. A change starts today and unused credits carry over (no proration in the default).
  • Confirm Payment shows Payment Successful; a declined card shows "Your card was declined, so nothing was charged" and Try again; Change opens the card form.

7. Backend needed

  • Monthly Pro / Gold SKUs and the Weekly Pass SKU (prices, credits, checkout); Pro monthly US$25 (800 credits a month) and Gold monthly US$120 (6,000 a month) per the 2 Nov price sheet; the Weekly Pass US$2.99 and its XXX credits are still placeholders.
  • Fields: payment failed + grace end date, Weekly Pass end date, App Store billing.
