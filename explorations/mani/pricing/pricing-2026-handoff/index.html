Scope

  • Question discount pricing page is the only pricing page for now: yearly plans; closing asks "What stopped you?" and each answer has its own steps (10% off, Pro monthly, Weekly Pass, fix the deck, nothing).

  • Weekly Pass (replaces "Pro weekly"): one-off 7 days, every feature except collaboration, nothing renews; move to monthly or yearly any time.
  • One Team plan: unlimited seats, one shared credit pool, one price. No Pro Team / Gold Team, no credits dropdown.
  • Mobile (App Store, Google Play, mobile paywall) is out of scope for now.

Question discount pricing page

Plans, yearly only (US catalog)

No Basic in the US. Pro US$240 (5,000 credits), Gold US$1,200 (50,000 credits, Best for you), Team US$720. No Monthly switch on this page. Paid accounts see Current plan / Change plan / Not available on your plan on the cards.

What stopped you?

Free accounts, first close of the plans only (closing again just closes). One tap moves on, X skips the rest. File: QuestionCloseFlowV2.jsx.

The price is too high: 10% off the first year

Pro or Gold yearly at 10% off the first year (coupon PRESENT10, CLOSE_COUPONS.pricing). Apply opens checkout for the selected plan. No thanks goes to Pro monthly.

Pro monthly step

Also the first step for 'I don't want to pay for a whole year' and the second after 'credits'. Price and credits are placeholders (US$25, XXX credits) until the monthly SKU exists.

Weekly Pass step (last step)

Weekly Pass: its own one-off plan, 7 days, every feature except team collaboration, nothing renews. US$2.99 and XXX credits are placeholders. Other answers: 'deck isn't good enough' shows Fix the deck first (Deck Score, Edit the outline, Hire an expert, no discount); 'just looking' shows nothing.

Monthly plans

Manage your plan: Pro monthly to Gold monthly

Opens from every Change plan (settings, pricing cards, dashboard nudges, out of credits, Gold features). Current plan card on top (no radio unless a yearly plan can renew early), Monthly / Yearly switch, checkout-style plan cards with the plan's points inside the selected card, one-click payment on the right. File: ManagePlanDialogV2.jsx.

Manage your plan: Pro monthly to Pro yearly

Yearly shows 'Save 20%'; Pro yearly carries 'Save US$60 a year', Gold '10x Pro'. Rules: up only, no yearly to monthly. Plan changes start today; unused credits carry over.

Manage your plan: Team

'Managing a team? Switch to a Team plan' text link (no tabs). One Team plan, one price (US$720 a year), no credits dropdown. 'Switch back to individual plans' returns.

Weekly Pass

Choose your new plan (from a Weekly Pass)

Current card: Weekly Pass, one-off 7 days, ends date. Picks Pro / Gold monthly or yearly or Team. The pass is not upgraded: it runs to its end date, then ends.

Monthly plans

Pick a plan to come back (expired monthly)

Ended plan card on top, same plans to pick from, starts today.

Settings, Plan and billing: Pro monthly

US$25/month, monthly invoices, Change plan and Cancel renewal. Cancel renewal on monthly says 'next month' and has no 20% offer step (yearly only).

Weekly Pass

Settings, Plan and billing: Weekly Pass

'US$2.99 for 7 days', 'Ends on DATE. Nothing renews.' No Cancel renewal. 'Get a monthly or yearly plan' opens Choose your new plan.

Monthly plans

Pro monthly: save US$60 a year; Gold monthly: save US$240. Dismiss hides it for a week. Switch to yearly opens Manage your plan with the yearly plan selected. File: PlanNoticeBanner.jsx.
