Gold Button Options

Eight treatments for the Gold plan CTA, same size and copy as the v6.2 card button. References pulled from Mobbin: Tinder Gold, POF Prestige, Locket Gold, Mammoth Gold.

A · Current (v6.2)

Live

Figma flat gradient, top sheen, golden glow, shimmer sweep. Baseline for comparison.

B · Champagne Satin

Ref: Tinder Gold

Pale top fading to deep champagne, dark brown text. Quiet, premium, no motion.

C · Hotspot Glow

Ref: POF Prestige

Radial light hotspot upper left over a rich gold base. Feels lit, like brushed metal.

D · Bright Honey

Ref: Locket Gold

Flat saturated yellow gold, near-black text. Loudest option, maximum contrast.

E · Metallic Foil

Multi-stop foil gradient. Hover slides the foil across the button, like tilting a gold card.

F · Dark Luxe

Near-black button, gradient gold text, thin gold ring, soft shimmer. Shown on dark stage; works on the gold card too.

G · Flowing Gold

Gradient slowly drifts left and right, speeds up on hover. Alive without a shimmer bar.

H · Glow Pulse

Figma gradient with a breathing outer glow. Draws the eye with light, not movement on the surface.
