Member purchases: answers for the new structure

Your 16 questions, with the original recommendation in grey and our answer in color, fitted to the new sharing rules.

New structure this is based on: Basic, Pro and Gold are single-user plans (only the owner, plus guests). Team is the only plan with members, and it has unlimited licences. Roles: Owner, Admin, Member, Guest Editor, Guest Viewer. AI credits are one shared pool per workspace, and guests use it too. Admins can't open private docs.
Keep the recommendation Changed for the new structure No longer applies

Roles and scope

1Which roles can buy?

Recommended: Owner + Admin first, with Member behind a workspace setting. Guests excluded.
Our answer Basic, Pro, Gold: Owner only (there are no members). Team: Owner + Admin, and Members only if the setting in #2 is on. Guests never buy, on any plan. Members can buy only two things: AI credits and Hire an expert. Nothing on the plan or subscription. Why: single-user plans have no members, so the question only exists on Team.

2Can the owner turn it off per workspace?

Recommended: one toggle, off by default for existing workspaces.
Our answer One toggle, "Allow members to purchase", in Team settings only. Off by default for every workspace, new ones included. Only the Owner and Admins can change it. Why: Team is unlimited, so members join freely. Off by default stops surprise spending.

3Should member spend be capped?

Recommended: no cap in v1, plus owner notification.
Our answer Keep it: no cap in v1, and the owner is emailed on every member purchase (#15). Add a cap later if abuse shows up.

Who pays

4Credits and hire-a-team: whose card?

Recommended: (a) the member's own card, with a new checkout.
Our answer Keep (a): members always pay with their own card. The owner's saved card is never shown to members.

5Plan upgrades: whose card?

Recommended: (a) the member sends an upgrade request, and the owner approves and pays.
Our answer Mostly no longer applies. Members only exist on Team, and Team is the top plan with unlimited licences, so there's nothing to upgrade to. Plan changes stay Owner and Admin only. If a member asks for a change (for example yearly billing), it goes to the owner as a request, as in (a).

6Who owns invoices and receipts for a member's purchase?

Recommended: the member gets the receipt and the owner gets a copy.
Our answer Keep: the member gets the receipt and the Owner gets a copy. Admins can see it in billing history (#16).

7Refunds and chargebacks on a member's purchase

Open question: who can ask, and which credits are clawed back, since the credits sit in the shared pool.
Our answer Who can ask: the member who paid, or the Owner. Where the money goes: always back to the card or account that paid. Clawback: only the unspent part of what that member bought is removed from the pool. Credits already used by anyone, guests included, aren't refunded. If the pool is already lower than the refund, the refund is reduced to match. Chargeback: same rule, and the member loses purchase rights until the Owner turns them back on. Why: the pool is shared and guests spend from it too, so you can't trace which credit was whose.

What a member can change

8Which subscription changes are allowed?

Recommended: tier upgrade and seat add. Downgrades stay owner-only.
Our answer No longer applies. Seats: Team is unlimited, so there are none to add. Tier: Team is already the top plan. Members change nothing on the subscription. Cycle changes and downgrades are Owner and Admin only.

9Trial eligibility: whose history counts?

Open question: the member's trial history or the owner's.
Our answer The workspace's history counts (the owner's). A trial belongs to the workspace, not to the person clicking. A member can still use their own trial in their personal workspace. Why: otherwise every new member of a Team could restart a trial for the same workspace.

10Coupons: can members use them?

Question: can members use coupons or retention codes.
Our answer Members can apply coupons to their own purchases (credits, hire-a-team). Retention and subscription coupons are Owner and Admin only, because members can't change the subscription (#8).

Hire a team / exports

11What deck access does a member need?

Recommended: decks they can edit.
Our answer Keep: decks they can edit. That means any deck in a Public Project, plus private decks where they're Editor. Guest Editors can edit but can't order, because guests never buy (#1).

12Who sees an expert-design order?

Open question: buyer only, owner too, or everyone in the workspace.
Our answer Only the buyer sees the order and the delivered file, in their own Downloads section. The same goes for exports: only the person who exported sees the exported file, even when the deck is in a Public Project. Owner and Admins: only the billing line (price, date, buyer), never the file. Example: Sara exports "Sales Pitch" from 🌎 Marketing. The PDF shows only in Sara's Downloads. Bala can open the deck, but he doesn't see Sara's export.

Credits

13Where do member-bought credits go?

Recommended: the shared workspace pool, and say so clearly on the purchase screen.
Our answer Keep: the shared pool. The purchase screen says "Credits go to the Foursquare pool. Everyone in the workspace, including guests, can use them."

14What happens if the member leaves the workspace?

Recommended: credits stay in the workspace.
Our answer Keep: credits stay in the workspace pool. No refund on leaving. This matches the member-removed rule, where shared things stay.

Visibility and audit

15What does the owner get told?

Recommended: email on every purchase, plus an audit log.
Our answer Keep: email the Owner on every member purchase, plus a purchase log in Settings → Billing that Owner and Admins can see.

16Can members see billing history?

Open question: their own orders only, or the full workspace history.
Our answer Members: their own orders only. Owner and Admins: the full workspace history. Guests: nothing.
