Member purchases: answers for the new structure

Card rules follow Figma and Canva. Your 16 questions, with the original recommendation in grey and our answer in color, fitted to the new sharing rules.

New structure this is based on: Basic, Pro and Gold are single-user plans (only the owner). Team is the only plan with members, and it has unlimited licences. Roles: Owner, Admin, Member (Editor or Commenter). No guests. View-only sharing is the public link. AI credits are one shared pool per workspace. Admins can't open private docs.
Keep the recommendation Changed for the new structure No longer applies

Roles and scope

1Which roles can buy?

Recommended: Owner + Admin first, with Member behind a workspace setting.
Our answer updated Basic, Pro, Gold: Owner only (no members). Team: everyone. Owner, Admins and Members can all buy AI credits and Hire an expert, anytime, with no limit on orders. Members can't buy anything on the plan or subscription.

2Can the owner turn it off per workspace?

Recommended: one toggle, off by default for existing workspaces.
Our answer updated No longer needed. There's no "Member purchases" setting: every member can always buy, on their own card.

3Should member spend be capped?

Recommended: no cap in v1, plus owner notification.
Our answer updated No cap. Members pay with their own card, so there's no risk to the workspace card. The Owner is emailed on every purchase, and everything shows in the purchase history (#15).

Who pays

4Credits and hire-a-team: whose card?

Recommended: (a) the member's own card, with a new checkout.
Our answer updated Owner and Admins: the workspace card. Every other member: their own card, always. Members never see the workspace card.

5Plan upgrades: whose card?

Recommended: (a) the member sends an upgrade request, and the owner approves and pays.
Our answer Mostly no longer applies. Members only exist on Team, and Team is the top plan with unlimited licences, so there's nothing to upgrade to. Plan changes are Owner only (see A2 below). If a member or Admin asks for a change (for example yearly billing), it goes to the owner as a request, as in (a).

6Who owns invoices and receipts for a member's purchase?

Recommended: the member gets the receipt and the owner gets a copy.
Our answer updated The buyer gets the receipt and the Owner gets a copy. Every purchase, on any card, shows in the purchase history for the Owner and Admins.

7Refunds and chargebacks on a member's purchase

Open question: who can ask, and which credits are clawed back, since the credits sit in the shared pool.
Our answer updated Who can ask: the buyer, or the Owner, by emailing support (A5). Where the money goes: always back to the card that paid (the workspace card for Owner and Admins, the member's own card for everyone else). Clawback: only the unspent part of what was bought is removed from the pool. Credits already used by anyone aren't refunded. Chargeback on a member's own card: that member's purchase rights are paused until the Owner turns them back on.

What a member can change

8Which subscription changes are allowed?

Recommended: tier upgrade and seat add. Downgrades stay owner-only.
Our answer No longer applies. Seats: Team is unlimited, so there are none to add. Tier: Team is already the top plan. Members change nothing on the subscription. Cycle changes are Owner only. Team can't be downgraded to a single-user plan.

9Trial eligibility: whose history counts?

Open question: the member's trial history or the owner's.
Our answer The workspace's history counts (the owner's). A trial belongs to the workspace, not to the person clicking. A member can still use their own trial in their personal workspace. Why: otherwise every new member of a Team could restart a trial for the same workspace.

10Coupons: can members use them?

Question: can members use coupons or retention codes.
Our answer Members can apply coupons to their own purchases (credits, hire-a-team). Retention and subscription coupons are Owner and Admin only, because members can't change the subscription (#8).

Hire a team / exports

11What deck access does a member need?

Recommended: decks they can edit.
Our answer Keep: decks they can edit. That means any deck in a Public Project, plus private decks where they're Editor.

12Who sees an expert-design order?

Open question: buyer only, owner too, or everyone in the workspace.
Our answer Only the buyer sees the order and the delivered file, in their own Downloads section. The same goes for exports: only the person who exported sees the exported file, even when the deck is in a Public Project. Owner and Admins: only the billing line (price, date, buyer), never the file. Example: Sara exports "Sales Pitch" from 🌎 Marketing. The PDF shows only in Sara's Downloads. Bala can open the deck, but he doesn't see Sara's export.

Credits

13Where do member-bought credits go?

Recommended: the shared workspace pool, and say so clearly on the purchase screen.
Our answer Keep: the shared pool. The purchase screen says "Credits go to the Foursquare pool. Everyone in the workspace can use them."

14What happens if the member leaves the workspace?

Recommended: credits stay in the workspace.
Our answer Keep: credits stay in the workspace pool. No refund on leaving. This matches the member-removed rule, where shared things stay.

Visibility and audit

15What does the owner get told?

Recommended: email on every purchase, plus an audit log.
Our answer Keep: email the Owner on every member purchase, plus a purchase log in Settings → Billing that Owner and Admins can see.

16Can members see billing history?

Open question: their own orders only, or the full workspace history.
Our answer updated Members: their own orders only. Owner and Admins: the full purchase history (like Canva).

Open questions, round 2 new

A1When an Admin buys credits or hire-an-expert, whose card pays?

Open question.
Our answer updated The workspace card, like Figma and Canva. The Admin doesn't use their own card. The Owner gets an email for every Admin purchase.

A2Do Admins get cancel, billing country and payment method?

Open question.
Our answer updated Payment method: Owner and Admins can add or replace the card (like Figma and Canva). Owner only: cancel, billing country and billing cycle, because they change the subscription and taxes. Admins also: view invoices and purchase history.

A3Which plans count as having members?

Open question.
Our answer Team only. Basic, Pro and Gold are single-user plans.

A4Does a member's chargeback suspend the workspace?

Open question.
Our answer No. Only that member is affected: their unspent bought credits are clawed back and their purchase rights are paused until the Owner turns them back on. The workspace is suspended only if the Owner's subscription payment is charged back.

A5How are refunds requested?

Open question.
Our answer updated By email to support only. No refund button or form in the app. The buyer or the Owner emails support, and support handles every refund and dispute. The money goes back to the card that paid (#7).

A6Should existing exports become private retroactively?

Open question.
Our answer Yes. The new rule applies to every export, old ones too: each export shows only in its exporter's Downloads. Show a one-time note: "Exports are now private to the person who made them." Anyone who can open the deck can export it again. Why: two rules for old and new exports would confuse people and leave old files exposed.

A7Should the legacy billing routes get the same gates?

Open question.
Our answer Yes, all of them. Every checkout and billing route checks the same rules on the server: who can buy, whose card, and what they can buy. Better still, redirect the legacy routes to the new checkout. Why: if one old route skips the check, members can go around every rule on this page.

A8Which gateways at launch?

Paddle and Razorpay still need a check that checkout works without a saved customer.
Our answer updated Owner and Admin purchases use the workspace card's saved customer, so they work on every gateway at launch. Member purchases use their own card, so they need the Paddle and Razorpay check. Until it passes, members in those regions see "Ask an Admin to buy this for you".
