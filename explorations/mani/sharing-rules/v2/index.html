3 kinds of project, folder or file

🔒 Private
  • Only the creator
  • Not even the workspace Owner or an Admin
  • Stays private until the creator shares it
  • Exception: a widget turned on earlier keeps working
👥 Shared with a few people
  • Creator + the people added (e.g. Madhu and Sumanth)
  • Folder access covers every doc and sub-folder inside, including new ones
  • One doc inside can be more open, e.g. public for the team. The team then sees only that doc
  • Anything moved out loses that access
🌎 Public
  • Every workspace Member (edit) and workspace Viewer (read-only)
  • New joiners see it too
  • Folder or file Viewers don't see it

People

👑 Owner

  • Created the workspace
  • Must transfer ownership to an Admin before leaving
  • Everything an Admin can do
  • Can't see others' private items

🛠 Admin

  • Manages members, billing and removals
  • Turns "Members can invite" on or off, and approves invite requests
  • Buys credits and Hire an expert in one click on the workspace card
  • Approves or declines Members' purchase requests (dashboard and sidebar)
  • Adds or changes the workspace card
  • Makes other Admins. Can remove members and Admins, never the Owner
  • Deletes whole projects and folders
  • Owns shared docs of people who leave (the Owner does if there are no Admins)
  • Can't see others' private items

👤 Member

  • Sees and edits every public item
  • Creates new docs
  • Deletes any doc they can edit (30-day restore). Deletes a whole project or folder only if they created it (they're its owner)
  • Moves docs between public projects. Only the creator makes a doc private
  • Turns the widget on or off
  • Invites anyone as a Viewer at any level, always straight away. Invites Members too. If "Members can invite" is off, Member invites wait for Owner or Admin approval
  • Download, export, duplicate
  • Uses the shared credit pool
  • Asks to buy credits or Hire an expert. The request goes to the Owner and Admins to approve. Can't add or change the card

👁 Workspace Viewer

  • Sees every public item, read-only
  • Can comment and present
  • Can copy a widget link that's already on
  • Can't create, edit, invite, download, export, duplicate or turn the widget on
  • No credits

👁 Folder or file Viewer

  • Only that project, folder or file, read-only
  • Can comment and present
  • Can't create, edit, invite, download, export or duplicate
  • Can copy a widget link that's already on
  • Shows in Settings → Members with an "Only: Sales folder" tag

Other rules

🔀 Moving

Access follows the folder. Warn on every move that changes access: private → public, public → private, and out of a shared folder ("Priya will lose access").

⬆️ Viewer → Member

The Owner, an Admin or any Member upgrades in one click. If "Members can invite" is off, a Member's upgrade becomes a request, like an invite. No 200 credits, because the account already exists.

⚖️ Two roles on one item

The higher role wins.

⚡ Removing access

Applies right away, even if the doc is open.

🚪 Member leaves

Public and shared docs stay, and an Admin becomes the owner (the Owner, if there are no Admins). Private docs are deleted: hidden 30 days, back only if the same person rejoins, then gone. Nobody else can open them.

🗑 Delete

Members delete any doc they can edit. Kept 30 days. The owner gets "Sara deleted Q4 Pitch. Restore?" and the trash shows "Deleted by Sara".

🪙 Credits

One shared pool per workspace. Free workspaces also get the invite bonus.

🗂 Projects and folders

Only the creator or an Admin deletes a whole project or folder. Others can "Leave" a shared one. Public projects can't be left.

📄 Duplicates

Copy goes next to the original if you can edit there, else your private space. Only slides are copied: no invites, comments or widget.

📥 Exports and Hire an expert

Only the person who exported or ordered sees the file. Hire an expert sends the expert a copy, never workspace access.

💳 Purchases

On paid plans: Owner and Admins buy in one click on the workspace card. Members send a request, which shows in the Owner's and Admins' dashboard and sidebar ("Bala wants to buy 5,000 credits"). Approve charges the workspace card. Only Owner and Admins can add or change the card. Free workspaces can't buy. The Owner is emailed on every purchase.

🙋 Purchase requests

Asked right where the Member hits the limit ("Ask an Admin for more credits"), with an optional note. Owner and Admins get an email with Approve plus an in-app alert. The Member sees Pending, Approved or Declined, and is emailed the decision. One pending request per item. Requests expire after 30 days. In a free workspace, Members send an upgrade request instead.

🪫 Credit pool

No per-person limit. The Owner is alerted when the pool is 80% used.

↩️ Refunds and payment

Refunds by email to support, back to the card that paid, unspent credits only. Paid plan payment fails: read-only right away. No downgrade from Team.

🔑 Who changes the plan

Owner and Admins upgrade the plan. Owner only: cancel, billing country and billing cycle. Members send an upgrade request instead.

🙋 Upgrade requests (free workspace)

Members see "Request upgrade" on paid features, and a top banner: "Only the Owner and Admins can upgrade". The Owner and Admins get "Madhu wants to use Brand kit. Please upgrade to Team" in-app and by email. Same inbox as other requests.

⬆️ Upgrading to Team

Members, files and roles carry over. Unused bonus credits stay. The invite bonus stops.

👤 Member → Viewer

The Owner or an Admin can do it. Bonus credits already earned stay.

👑 Owner transfer

To an Admin. For a free workspace, blocked if that person already owns one. Owner unreachable: contact support.

🏷 Workspace settings

"Members can invite": on by default. Off: Member invites wait for Owner or Admin approval (Free and Team). Team also has auto-join by company email (Admin picks Member or Viewer) and Block outside sharing, both off by default.

✉️ Invites

Expire after 14 days. Resend restarts the clock. Revoke anytime.

📥 Shared with me

A sidebar tab listing every folder and file shared with you, and who shared it.

🔔 Emails

Everyone: on share and on @mention. Owner and Admins: purchase, invite and upgrade requests (with Approve). The Member who asked: the decision. Owner: every purchase and the 80% credit alert. File owner: "Deleted by". Everyone: when the workspace is deleted. Everything else is in-app.

👯 Editing together

Live co-editing. Everyone sees each other's cursors.

🕘 Version history

Coming later.

👤 File owner

Whoever creates a file owns it for good. No handing over. Only exception: when someone leaves, an Admin (or the Owner) takes their shared docs.

✏️ Renaming

Only the creator or an Admin renames a project or folder.

🔁 Many workspaces

Fully separate: own files, members and credits. Switch from the top. Files can't move between workspaces.

💥 Deleting the workspace

Only the Owner, after typing its name. Paid plan cancelled, everyone emailed, kept 30 days, then gone.

Who can see and do what 🧒

The super simple version of v2. Every rule is here, in plain words.

🏠 1. It's like a house

🏠Workspacethe house
→
🚪Folder or projecta room (rooms can have rooms inside)
→
📄Filea page

Mani built the house called Formula One. So Mani is the boss (the Owner).

👥 2. The people

👑

Owner

Built the house. Can do everything a helper can. Must give the house to a helper before leaving.

🛠

Admin

The boss's helper. Adds and removes people, and can make more helpers. Can never remove the Owner.

✏️

Member

On the team. Sees every public room, changes things, makes new pages, invites people.

👀

Viewer

Only looks. Can leave comments and present. Can't change anything.

🙈 Nobody can see your private stuff. Not the Owner, not the helpers.

⭐ 3. The two golden rules

✏️ A Member always joins the whole house. Even if you invite them to one room, they see every public room.
👀 A Viewer only sees what you give them. One room = only that room. One page = only that page.

When you pick Member for one room, we warn you: "Bala will see all public rooms. Pick Viewer to share only this room."

🚪 4. Three kinds of rooms

🔒

Private

Only you.

👥

Shared

You plus the people you pick.

🌎

Public

Everyone in the house.

If a room is shared, everyone in it also sees new pages and rooms you add later. Take a page out of the room, and they lose it (we warn you first).

🪆 Rooms can have rooms inside them. An inside room or page can be more open than the room around it, but never more secret.

🎟 5. Plans

🆓

Free

One free house per person. Invite as many people as you want. Get 200 credits each time a new person makes an account, checks their email and joins. Up to 2,000. You can't buy more credits on Free. Upgrading later: alone = any plan, with other people = Team only.

👤

Basic, Pro, Gold

Just you. To invite anyone, upgrade to Team.

👥

Team

Invite everyone. The whole team shares one big pot of credits.

✉️ Who can invite

✏️

Members

Can invite new Members and Viewers. There's a switch called "Members can invite". It's on at the start.

🔘

Switch off?

👀

Viewers

Can't invite anyone.

👑 The Owner and 🛠 Admins can always invite anyone, and only they can flip the switch.
