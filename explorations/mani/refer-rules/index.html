Refer and earn v3

Example: Joe uses Presentations.AI. He sends his link to his friend Priya, who has never used it.

🎁 Rewards are credits onlyEvery reward is AI credits, added to the account. No cash, no payout, no points, no discount. Nothing to redeem or withdraw.

People

🆓 Joe on a Free plan

  • Shares his link from Refer and earn (side nav) or Settings > Rewards
  • ✅ +200 credits for each friend, after their first deck
  • ❌ No upgrade credits. The page shows them locked, with a Pro upsell

💎 Joe on a paid plan

  • Any paid plan counts: Standard, Pro or Team
  • ✅ +200 credits for each friend, after their first deck
  • ✅ +1,000 credits when a friend upgrades within 7 days and stays paid 30 days

🙋 Priya, the friend

  • Must be new to Presentations.AI
  • Gets her own workspace. She never joins Joe's
  • ✅ +200 credits after her first deck
  • Gets her own link right away, so she can refer friends too

Limits, per year (reset 1 Jan)

Sign up credits

2,000 credits, which is 10 friends
  • Sign ups from Joe's published Community templates count toward the same 2,000
  • After the limit, friends still get their 200. Joe gets 0

Upgrade credits (paid plan only)

10,000 credits, which is 10 upgrades
  • After the limit, the row says "Limit reached"
  • Each friend counts once, monthly or yearly

User stories

1. 🆓 Free Joe invites Priya

  1. Joe copies his link from Refer and earn and sends it on WhatsApp
  2. Priya signs up. Joe's table shows her as Signed up, credits "After their first deck"
  3. Priya makes her first deck. She sees "200 credits added!"
  4. Joe gets a toast and a bell alert: "Priya joined with your referral link. +200 credits"

Result: both have +200 credits.

2. 💎 Paid Joe, Priya upgrades

  1. Same as story 1, both get 200
  2. On day 3, Priya upgrades to Pro. Her upgrade screen says Joe gets a thank-you reward
  3. Joe sees "Priya upgraded. +1,000 credits after 30 paid days"
  4. Priya stays paid for 30 days

Result: Joe gets +1,000 credits. Upgrade credits this year: 1,000 / 10,000.

3. 🆓 Free Joe, Priya upgrades

  1. Priya signs up, makes a deck, upgrades on day 2
  2. Joe sees "Upgrade credits only count while you're on a paid plan"
  3. The page shows "Get 1,000 credits" locked, with a Pro upsell
  4. Joe upgrades now. Only friends who upgrade after this count

Result: Joe keeps the 200. No upgrade credits for Priya's upgrade.

4. 🚫 Joe hits the limit

  1. Joe has already earned 2,000 sign up credits this year
  2. Ravi, his 11th friend, signs up and makes a deck
  3. Ravi still gets his 200. Joe's row shows "0, Limit reached"
  4. On 1 Jan the limit resets

Result: the friend never loses out.

5. 🔁 Priya never makes a deck

  1. Joe emails his link to sam@northwind.co. Row shows Invited, "Waiting for sign up", with Send reminder
  2. Sam signs up but never makes a deck

Result: no credits for anyone until the first deck.

6. 🌍 Sign up from a Community template

  1. Joe publishes a template to Community. It carries his link
  2. Lena opens the template, signs up and makes a deck

Result: both get 200. It counts toward Joe's same 2,000 limit.

Quick answers

Can Joe get cash?No. Only credits.
Does Priya join Joe's workspace?No. She gets her own.
When do credits land?Sign up credits: at the friend's first deck. Upgrade credits: after 30 paid days.
How many friends can Joe invite?As many as he likes. Credits stop at 2,000 a year, friends still get theirs.
Does Free Joe earn anything?Yes, 200 per friend. Upgrade credits need a paid plan.
What about the old Figma (points, 20% off)?Replaced. Follow this page and the proto.
