Shortlist · 4 versions

Opening an old deck

What people see between clicking an older deck and being able to edit it: the status at the bottom (in the floater's slot) or in the top bar. Each replays from the start; use the bar at the bottom left to move between them.

Option 9a
Bottom · count
A card in the floater's slot names the deck and the step, with a 3/8 count. Becomes the floater when ready.
Option 9b
Bottom · segments
Same bottom card, with 8 small segments (one per slide) filling as each slide lands. Becomes the floater when ready.
Option 10a
Top bar · segments
The status sits centred in the top bar: deck name, step and 8 segments. When ready it fades and the header actions fill in on the right.
Option 10b
Top bar · line
Same top bar status with a 3/8 count, plus a thin progress line along the bottom edge of the whole header.
