Checkout & purchase flow.

Checkout
Checkout — exit-intent flow
Stitched journey: starts on the no-offer checkout; tapping the ✕ fires a small "before you go" nudge that, on accept, reveals the 50% discount and starts the 1-hour timer in place. Standalone (no page hop).
Checkout — offer + 1-hour timer
Two-pane checkout with a live discount (SALE tag, 50% off ribbon, discount line) and a 1-hour countdown. When the timer hits zero the offer ends and prices revert to standard.
Checkout — no offer
The plain state: standard plan pricing, no discount, badges, or timer. Pro / Gold radio cards, monthly ↔ annual, card / UPI payment.
